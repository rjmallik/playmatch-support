Privacy Policy — PlayMatch

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · Last updated: November 18, 2026

PlayMatch (“the App”) is a sports app that lets players create an account, set up a profile, find courts and fields, add friends, book time, and join pickup games. This policy explains what data the App collects and what happens to it. Contact: playmatchsupport@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address, username, display name, position, skill level, city, and sport. Your email is used to sign you in, send a password reset, and optionally send an account verification email. Your username, display name, position, skill level, city, and sport are visible to other signed-in users so they can find and add you as a friend.
  • Skill profile. You can add your age and height. Your height is shown on your profile so other players can see it; your age is never shown to anyone.
  • Location. When you use the map or look for courts near you, the App uses your device location to find nearby courts. Your device’s current location (precise coordinates) is sent to OpenStreetMap’s Overpass API to look up nearby courts; it is not stored on our servers. Courts you add yourself are public places and their location is stored so other players can find them.
  • Friends, bookings, games, and chat. Friend requests, friendships, court bookings, pickup games you post or join, group chat messages in those games, and 1-to-1 direct messages are stored so the App can show them to the people involved. Only players who joined a game can see or post in its group chat. Only the two participants in a direct chat can see its messages. If you block another player, that block is stored so it survives reinstalls; a blocked player cannot send you friend requests or direct messages, or join your games. If you report another player, the report is stored for review; reports are not shown inside the App.
  • Profile photos. The App does not store profile photos. Photo upload is not currently available, and no photo files are kept on our servers.
  • Identifiers. Your account has an internal user ID. It is not shared with advertisers or anyone else.

What we do not collect

The App has no ads and does not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The App does include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Performance Monitoring (see Third parties below), which collect app-usage, crash, and performance data. We do not use that data for advertising or tracking you across other apps.

Third parties

  • Firebase (Google). Accounts and app data are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Performance Monitoring are also included in the App: they collect app-usage, crash, and performance data, including device information and an app-instance identifier, to help fix crashes and improve the App. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy terms.
  • OpenStreetMap. Map data comes from OpenStreetMap; your device’s current location (precise coordinates) is sent to its Overpass API only to fetch nearby courts.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from inside the App (My Profile > Delete Account). Account deletion is done in the App; there is no email deletion-request process. Deleting your account deletes your sign-in, your profile, and your username reservation, freeing the username for reuse. It also deletes your friend requests and friendships, the bookings you made, the games you created (including their group chat messages), your 1-to-1 chats and messages, the blocks you created, and the reports you filed, and it removes you from games and bookings you joined. Courts you added stay on the map because they are public places. Reports filed about you may be kept for safety review; they are never shown inside the App. To ask about anything else, contact playmatchsupport@gmail.com.

Children

PlayMatch is not for children under 13; if you are under 13, do not use the App. If you are 13 to 17, use PlayMatch only with a parent or guardian’s permission and guidance.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the new policy.
