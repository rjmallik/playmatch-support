Support — PlayMatch

Help, bug reports, and player reports

Need help with PlayMatch, found a bug, or want to report a player? Email playmatchsupport@gmail.com, or in the App go to Profile > Contact and Support.

Common questions

How do I delete my account? In the App: Profile > Delete Account. This deletes your profile, the games you created, your chats, and your bookings.

How do I block or report someone? Open their profile or a chat with them and choose Block or Report. Blocked players can’t message you, send you friend requests, or join your games.

The map shows no courts near me. Allow location access for PlayMatch in Settings, or set your city in Edit Profile.
